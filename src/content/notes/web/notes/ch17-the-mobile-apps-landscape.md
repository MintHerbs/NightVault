# Chapter 17: The mobile apps landscape

*Week 17 · 2+1 hrs*

A quick zoom-out on where mobile apps sit today, useful context before you design one.

* Most people spend the majority of their phone time inside a small number of apps they already have installed, not browsing for new ones. Getting discovered is genuinely hard.
* Apps typically fall into a few broad categories: utility (get in, do a task, get out), social/communication, commerce, and entertainment. Know which one yours is, it shapes every design decision.
* Monetization models: free with ads, subscription, one-time purchase, or free with in-app purchases. This decision affects your backend too, e.g. a subscription needs an endpoint that checks active status.

## Where our recruitment app fits

Ares Colony's mobile app would be a utility app: someone opens it, checks seats or applies, leaves. No ads, no subscription, it's a straightforward extension of the same Django API.
